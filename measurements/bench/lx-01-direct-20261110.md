# L-X first live input — 2026-11-10

**Initial input demonstration succeeded.** The owner confirmed that the S3 Plan-page indication follows the left joystick. Serial acquisition also showed changing GPIO5 readings, including estimated values around1.49–1.54 V, with no ADC driver errors.

The owner selected a quick direct-contact test: common gamepad/S3 ground, GPIO5 probed onto L-X, **no series resistor and no capacitor**. This differs from the proposed RC circuit in LX-01. The S3 was USB-powered; gamepad battery-only operation with its charging USB disconnected was specified, but its source/charger state was not independently reconfirmed during capture.

Before the L-X attempt, an owner-confirmed direct GPIO5-to-S3-GND check recorded near-zero readings for5.801 seconds and passed the zero-reference diagnostic. This did not calibrate the full ADC range.

The final observer record contains1756 reports over179.82 seconds. Acquisition advanced without stale-sample or driver-error failures; the recorded maximum loop work remained16.937 ms and microphone error count remained0. Sampling was explicitly stopped at the end and inactive state was verified. Gamepad control and radio remained disabled.

The capture includes open/handled probe intervals and unmarked joystick movement. Its raw range0–4095 is **not** a measured low/high stick calibration; saturation/contact effects are not distinguished. Whole-record variation is not held-center noise. Values in mV are ADC estimates rather than new independent DMM measurements.

The [sanitized structured result](lx-01-direct-20261110.json) records the evidence and limits. Full runtime logs remain private. This demonstrates the first L-X-to-S3 signal path and visible response. It does not qualify powered-off operation, a permanent interface, repeat-center accuracy, all buttons/axes or a custom harness.

Remove the signal probe before either board is reset, unplugged or powered off. The permanent power/interface design remains a later gate.
