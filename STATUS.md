# Project status — 2026-11-13

- The owner approved the [single-pack power design](electronics/POWER_SYSTEM.md) and existing EGSCST switch. The HiLetgo protected output powers the original gamepad through its donor lead and the two ESP boards through the XL63020 at 3.3 V. Charging uses the HiLetgo USB-C port with the handheld off. Fuse and wire sizing, perfboard layout and physical validation remain pending.

**First L-X input demonstrated:** the owner confirmed the S3 display follows the left stick during a direct temporary probe test without a resistor/capacitor. The serial record showed responsive acquisition, no ADC errors and healthy microphone acquisition; sampling was stopped. See the [dated result](measurements/bench/lx-01-direct-20261110.md). This is an initial signal-path milestone, not full LX-01 acceptance or a permanent harness/interface qualification.

The [LX-01 plan](docs/TEMP_INPUT_TEST.md) defines the proposed RC interface and staged checks. The separate `bench-axis-test` build provides explicit start/stop, raw/filtered telemetry, estimated mV and a five-minute sampling limit. Initial visible response is confirmed; formal repeatability, DMM comparison and input loading checks remain pending. Neither a direct probe nor the proposed resistor/capacitor input is power-off-safe: remove the signal before reset/upload/power changes. Permanent protection and mechanical fit remain separate release gates.

- Bench firmware 0.1.1 exercises display, touch and microphone on ESP32-S3, with 16 MB flash and 8 MB PSRAM. The corrected interface was confirmed smooth and responsive. Radio and actuator control remain unimplemented.
- Main PCB readings: width125.5 mm, height62.5 mm, thickness1.15 mm, hole A diameter 5 mm (restated by the owner; 4.83 mm was reported first). All mechanical readings are owner-reported and taken with the owner's AOCORIC 09-15473 digital caliper; its uncertainty is not recorded. The datum and the readings for holes B to F are recorded as well. The canonical record is `measurements/boards/gamepad-gh-wii-u002.json`.
- Native FreeCAD r005 (`cad/boards/gamepad-gh-wii-u002/`) is the real board shape: a **traced contour** with holes A to F cut in and **83 traced test pads** on the rear surface (table: `measurements/boards/gamepad-gh-wii-u002-pads.csv`; labelled plan view: `exports/boards/gamepad-gh-wii-u002/gamepad-gh-wii-u002-pad-map-REAR.png`), rear (component) surface at Z0. The contour was read from two rectified owner photographs at about ±0.4 mm, with four short stretches bridged where a ribbon, the battery connector or the USB shell hides the edge. Datum: bottom-left of the bounding rectangle in the rear view, at the battery-connector end. Components, connectors and daughterboards are not modeled, and loose daughterboards have independent mounting positions. r002 (envelope only), r003 (outline and holes, no pads) and r004 (67 pads) are kept for history. r005 adds the round button test pads near SW1 (H, K-+, K--, L-1, L-2) and other test points, and reads three of the pads beside the left-stick ribbon as L-3, L-X and L-Y from zoomed photos; the round M+ and M- motor pads are placed at about ±1 mm.
- Ten hardware photos, a gallery and a measurement guide are available under `measurements/photos/gamepad-gh-wii-u002/`. Perspective photos establish identity/layout, not scale.
- The synthetic toolchain fixture is separate from controller geometry. See `cad/toolchain-smoke/` and `exports/toolchain-smoke/`.
- Owner bench readings now show joystick axes spanning approximately 0–3 V with ~1.5 V center, example buttons active-low (L-3, R-3, L-2 shoulder, one unnamed face button), and ~3 V logic remaining present after pairing LEDs stop. A DIY logic analyzer reported no observable communication on large `SCL`/`SDA` pads during the sampled startup and failed-pairing window; this is not proof of no bus. See the dated electrical bench notes under `measurements/boards/` and `measurements/electrical.csv`.

**Harness package (proposal):** [docs/HARNESS_PLAN.md](docs/HARNESS_PLAN.md) proposes seven JST XH signal connectors (2.5 mm pitch, 3 to 5 positions each, from the owner's stock) that land only on free round test pads: left stick and right stick (to the S3), and face buttons, right shoulders, D-pad, left shoulders and centre buttons (to an ESP32-WROOM-32U on a DevKitC V4, which reads the buttons and sends their state to the S3 over a UART). They are drawn on the [tap map](exports/boards/gamepad-gh-wii-u002/gamepad-gh-wii-u002-harness-taps-REAR.png). Twenty signals are plan-ready; Minus has two candidate pads, POWER is held until its voltage is measured, and the ground references need one continuity check. Voltage levels are on record (about 3 V logic, active-low buttons; L-3, R-3 and L-2 measured individually); the other 13 button pads are named from the printed labels; by the owner's decision they are confirmed at the first read through the button processor, not pad by pad beforehand. Power and the SDA/SCL pads stay on hold.

**Harness drawings and 3D model (proposed, not for assembly):** WireViz drawings for each connector and an [overview of all seven](electronics/generated/overview-proposed.png) are rendered under `electronics/generated/`; they show the pad, wire ID, housing position and far-end pin of every wire. The far-end pins are the registry's proposals (movable; the button processor's pin list is not yet checked against its datasheet). Wire colours, gauge, lengths, housing part numbers and the board-side headers are not decided and are drawn as such. The first 3D harness model, `cad/harness/gamepad-harness-r001.FCStd`, is the traced board with a marker on each of the 24 pads the 28 wires land on (or may land on) ([view](exports/harness/gamepad-harness-r001-TAPS-AXO.png)); it has no wires, housings or other modules yet, because the S3, display, microphone and button-processor board are not measured or placed. Ground returns: the owner reports the large VSS pad (TP03) is ground, checked against the large GND pad (TP01), so the left-hand connectors return to TP03 and the right-hand ones to TP01; the small 1.2 mm GND pad is no longer used.

**Module models (first readings in):** the owner's readings of the S3 board, display and microphone (2026-11-12) are recorded in `measurements/modules/*-20261112.json`. The S3 is placed in placement r002 and the display, battery (65 x 36 x 10 mm nominal) and microphone are parked reference bodies. They cannot all share one layer inside the gamepad's footprint, so the layout (D5 in docs/HARNESS_PLAN.md) is the owner's next decision. The S3 header order and pitch are assumed, not measured. The owner chose to keep the gamepad in its original shell and add a printed pod for the display, both ESP boards and the power parts; `cad/enclosure/pod-r001` is a first space claim (116.8 x 72.8 x 38.8 mm, several heights assumed, no mount), not a printable part. The readings it needs are listed in `measurements/templates/pod-readings.csv`. The caliper sheets in `measurements/templates/` remain for the readings still missing.

Next mechanical work: caliper spot-checks of the traced contour (the far-end diagonal's legs and the notch beside the SYNC switch); connector and pad-group positions (`measurements/boards/gamepad-gh-wii-u002-features.csv`), component heights, USB overhang, daughterboard envelopes, battery and ribbon routing, toward the integrated assembly described in `docs/PROJECT_PLAN.md`. Next electrical work: validate power-sequencing/off-state backfeed and the proposed **single L-X observer input** with a current-limited high-impedance interface; then expand button sampling and the harness. Do not connect original PCB power rails to S3 3V3. See the open issues and docs/PROJECT_PLAN.md.
