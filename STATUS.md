# Project status — 2026-11-10

**First L-X input demonstrated:** the owner confirmed the S3 display follows the left stick during a direct temporary probe test without a resistor/capacitor. The serial record showed responsive acquisition, no ADC errors and healthy microphone acquisition; sampling was stopped. See the [dated result](measurements/bench/lx-01-direct-20261110.md). This is an initial signal-path milestone, not full LX-01 acceptance or a permanent harness/interface qualification.

The [LX-01 plan](docs/TEMP_INPUT_TEST.md) defines the proposed RC interface and staged checks. The separate `bench-axis-test` build provides explicit start/stop, raw/filtered telemetry, estimated mV and a five-minute sampling limit. Initial visible response is confirmed; formal repeatability, DMM comparison and input loading checks remain pending. Neither a direct probe nor the proposed resistor/capacitor input is power-off-safe: remove the signal before reset/upload/power changes. Permanent protection and mechanical fit remain separate release gates.

- Bench firmware 0.1.1 exercises display, touch and microphone on ESP32-S3, with 16 MB flash and 8 MB PSRAM. The corrected interface was confirmed smooth and responsive. Radio and actuator control remain unimplemented.
- Main PCB readings: width125.5 mm, height62.5 mm, thickness1.15 mm, hole A diameter4.83 mm. These are owner-reported measurements; instrument and uncertainty were not supplied. The canonical record is `measurements/boards/gamepad-gh-wii-u002.json`.
- Native FreeCAD r002 contains a rectangular bounding envelope only, top Z0, volume9020.3125 mm³, no hole cuts. Actual contour, hole positions and component clearances remain unmeasured. Loose daughterboards have independent mounting positions.
- Ten hardware photos, a gallery and a measurement guide are available under `measurements/photos/gamepad-gh-wii-u002/`. Perspective photos establish identity/layout, not scale.
- The synthetic toolchain fixture is separate from controller geometry. See `cad/toolchain-smoke/` and `exports/toolchain-smoke/`.
- Owner bench readings now show joystick axes spanning approximately 0–3 V with ~1.5 V center, example buttons active-low (L-3, R-3, L-2 shoulder, one unnamed face button), and ~3 V logic remaining present after pairing LEDs stop. A DIY logic analyzer reported no observable communication on large `SCL`/`SDA` pads during the sampled startup and failed-pairing window; this is not proof of no bus. See the dated electrical bench notes under `measurements/boards/` and `measurements/electrical.csv`.

Next mechanical work: agree a physical datum; measure the contour, hole centers, component heights, USB overhang and ribbon routing. Next electrical work: validate power-sequencing/off-state backfeed and the proposed **single L-X observer input** with a current-limited high-impedance interface; then expand button sampling and the harness. Do not connect original PCB power rails to S3 3V3. See the open issues and docs/PROJECT_PLAN.md.
