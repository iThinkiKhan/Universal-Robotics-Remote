# Component record

The canonical inventory and its confidence/evidence are in ../hardware/components.json. Signals are in ../hardware/signals.json, connector plans in ../electronics/harnesses.json, and readings/photos in ../measurements/.

Before modeling a real board, record its outline, thickness, hole centers, component/connector envelopes, reference datum and source evidence. The original gamepad stays intact and is represented as a black-box electrical module until its interface is measured. Do not infer voltages or physical dimensions from labels or synthetic toolchain geometry.
